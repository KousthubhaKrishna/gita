# Privacy Policy — భగవద్గీత (Bhagavad Gita, Telugu)

**Developer:** KodeSweep · **Contact:** kodesweep@gmail.com · **Effective:** 23 November 2025

## Summary

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It works entirely offline.

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data **only on your device**, so that it can remember your place and preferences:

- which verses you have read, your bookmarks, and your reading streak;
- display settings such as theme, text size, text weight, chosen image style, and which translations are shown.

This information never leaves your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can reset reading progress at any time from the app's settings.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- No network requests. All texts, translations, commentary, fonts and images are bundled inside the app.
- No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other device permission beyond what Android grants every app by default.
- No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.

## Children

The app contains religious and literary text and is suitable for all ages. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that handles personal data, this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.

## Open source

The complete source code is public at https://github.com/KousthubhaKrishna/gita, so anyone can verify the statements above.
