## English

**App name** (24/30)
Bhagavad Gita – Telugu

**Short description** (78/80)
All 700 verses with word meanings, two Telugu translations and commentary. Offline.

**Full description**
A quiet, offline Bhagavad Gita for Telugu readers. One verse per page, the way a printed Gita reads. No internet, no account, no ads.

What's inside
• All 18 chapters and 700 slokas in Telugu script
• Word-by-word meanings (pratipadartham) for every verse
• Two Telugu translations: Swami Mukundananda's, and Swamini Saradapriyananda's (Chinmaya Mission)
• Full commentary on every verse
• Optional Devanagari text, IAST transliteration and English translation

Reading
• Swipe through a chapter one sloka at a time; the app remembers where you stopped
• Read-verse tracking, bookmarks, a daily streak and per-chapter progress
• A "verse of the day" that walks the whole text over time

Design
• Choose classical Indian miniature paintings (public domain) or photographs of Gita sites
• Noto Serif Telugu typography, five text sizes, three weights, light and dark themes

Privacy
The app collects nothing and sends nothing. Your reading progress stays on your phone.

Sources
Verses, word meanings, translation and commentary: Swami Mukundananda, holy-bhagavad-gita.org (copyright the author; for personal reading). Second translation: te.wikisource.org (CC BY-SA 4.0). Devanagari, IAST and English: the nvkudva/Bhagavad-Geeta compilation. Fonts: Noto (OFL). Artwork: Wikimedia Commons and The Met, public domain / CC. Full credits in the app under "Sources & acknowledgements" and at https://github.com/KousthubhaKrishna/gita.
